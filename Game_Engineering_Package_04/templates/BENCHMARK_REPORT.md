# Benchmark report

## Question and hypothesis
Define the exact workload and metric before measuring.

## Correctness
Shared semantic contract, independent expected results/checksum, excluded cases.

## Environment
CPU/OS/compiler/standard library/build flags/configuration; background work.

## Method
Dataset sizes/seed; setup and timing boundaries; warmup/order; repeated samples.

## Results
Attach raw CSV. Report central tendency and dispersion, units and sample count.

## Interpretation
Supported conclusion, surprising/negative result, limitations and changed-workload prediction. Do not call a faster incorrect output an improvement.
