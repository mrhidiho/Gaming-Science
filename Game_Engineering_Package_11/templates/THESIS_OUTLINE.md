# Thesis outline

Title / author / version / reviewed question: [enter]

## Abstract
Question, method, primary result and main limitation.

## 1. Introduction
Motivation, precise problem, contributions and exclusions.

## 2. Related work
Closest method, baseline, differences and unresolved gap with accurate citations.

## 3. System and assumptions
Interface, workloads, model fidelity, physical/tool environment and correctness contract.

## 4. Method
Implemented mechanism, alternatives and versioned design decisions.

## 5. Correctness and verification
Oracle, tests, proofs/assumptions, adverse cases and uncovered guarantees.

## 6. Evaluation
Frozen protocol, main comparison, ablation, sensitivity, raw evidence, failed cases and uncertainty.

## 7. Discussion and limitations
Threats to validity, generalization boundary, negative findings and practical implications.

## 8. Conclusion
Only claims actually established and the next bounded question.

## Appendices
Reproduction guide, detailed derivations/configs/tables, notices and review records. Plan around 30–60 pages plus appendices; do not pad to a quota.
