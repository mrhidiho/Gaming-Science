The original volume identifiers and assignment metadata come from the supplied program manifest. Primary-source readings are linked rather than redistributed as textbooks or toolchains.

Third-party tools, libraries, platforms and referenced works remain subject to their own licenses and terms. The package does not grant rights to restricted process-design kits, proprietary traces, commercial games or someone else’s code. Review rights before publication or redistribution of a learner artifact.

The code is educational and intentionally bounded. Student functions are unfinished by design; optional EDA inputs require local verification. No warranty of production fitness, hardware safety, security assurance, academic credit, fabrication readiness or research novelty is implied. A claim should never be broader than the actual evidence recorded in the relevant validation or learner review.

No font files, compiler binaries, external toolchains or third-party textbooks are bundled. See `validation/VALIDATION_REPORT.md` for the actual executed tests and remaining limitations.
