# Research thesis outline

Working title / primary specialization:
Research question and falsifiable hypothesis:
Advisor or qualified reviewer:

## Proposal
Motivation, related work, gap, baseline, method, metrics, reproducibility plan, resource/ethical/license constraints, milestones, risks and fallback.

## Thesis chapters
1. Abstract and contribution statement.
2. Problem, scope and assumptions.
3. Related work and distinctions.
4. Method and derivation.
5. Implementation and validation.
6. Experiments, controls and ablations.
7. Results, uncertainty and limitations.
8. Conclusions, negative results and future questions.

## Artifact
Source, environment, data provenance, commands, tests, raw measurements, analysis and expected results.

## Defense
State what the evidence establishes and what it does not. Reproduce one central result live; explain failure cases and alternative designs.
