Status: proposed, accepted or superseded.

## Context and constraints
User needs, workload, target platform, budgets, compatibility, licensing and risks.

## Options
Compare at least two plausible options and the baseline, with assumptions.

## Evidence
Tests, measurements, models and their limitations.

## Decision and rationale
Explain the tradeoff rather than claiming universal superiority.

## Consequences
Maintenance cost, failure behavior, security/accessibility effects, rejected alternatives.

## Revisit trigger
What observation would change this choice?
