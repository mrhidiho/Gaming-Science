## Critical checks
Required functionality; no known critical safety/security issue; no unexplained test failures; reproducible build; honest limitations and provenance. Every critical check must pass.

## Defense questions
Explain a core design; trace an edge case; identify a tradeoff; propose and test a change.

## Decision and repair
