# Evidence and terminology quick reference

The definitions below are concise course reminders. Consult the assigned primary sources for formal assumptions and complete derivations.

### Ablation

A controlled removal or replacement intended to isolate a component's contribution.

### Arithmetic intensity

Useful operations per byte moved at a specified memory level; the level and counting convention matter.

### Condition number

A measure of sensitivity of a mathematical problem; for invertible A in the 2-norm, ||A||2*||A^-1||2.

### Critical path

The longest dependent chain in the task graph, including required waits and movement.

### Disocclusion

A surface becomes newly visible, making some prior pixel history invalid.

### Evidence tier

An explicit label distinguishing derivation, analytical model, software experiment, device measurement and independent review.

### Fencing token

An advancing authority version checked at the receiver that commits an effect, under a specified coordination protocol.

### Forward error

Distance between a computed solution and the true/reference solution, distinct from equation residual.

### Identifiability

Whether the observations distinguish the unknown parameters under the chosen model.

### Independent trial unit

The run, scene, device or other unit treated as independent for a specified inference.

### KKT conditions

Primal/dual feasibility, stationarity and complementary slackness; necessity and sufficiency depend on assumptions.

### Legality

Preservation of the accepted language's observable behavior under a stated transformation contract.

### Liveness

An eventual-progress property under the relevant environment assumptions.

### Multiple importance sampling

Combination of sampling strategies with weights/densities that match the estimator and common measure.

### Phi node

SSA merge representation that selects an incoming value according to the predecessor edge.

### Preconditioner

An auxiliary operation intended to improve iterative convergence while preserving the target system when correctly applied.

### Residual

Equation mismatch, such as b-A*x, with an explicitly stated norm.

### Safety

Exclusion of forbidden behavior; a finite bad prefix can demonstrate a violation.

### Span

Longest dependency chain of an algorithm under a defined computation model.

### Specialization

Use of known parameters to simplify a computation, trading runtime work for preparation/variant costs.

### SSA

Static single assignment: each represented value has one defining assignment, with merge semantics made explicit.

### Truncation bias

A change in the target expectation caused by an uncompensated finite cutoff or approximation.

### Work

Total operation count under the selected algorithmic model, not elapsed time.

### XPBD

A compliant constraint formulation with timestep-scaled compliance and accumulated multipliers; its assumptions and discretization remain important.
